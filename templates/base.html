🌐 About Cozy Weather & Live Earth

Cozy Weather is an advanced environmental intelligence platform providing real-time weather analytics, 5-day forecasts, clothing suggestions, air quality monitoring, and Live Earth observation capabilities.

🌍 Live Earth Features

  • Public & Traffic Cameras: Integrated official public camera directories for cities, highways, airports, and harbors.
  • Weather Satellite Imagery: Real-time cloud cover, rain radar (RainViewer), temperature, and wind velocity overlays.
  • Earth Observation: Daily high-resolution satellite imagery snapshots from NASA GIBS (Terra MODIS / VIIRS).
  • Favorites System: Save and bookmark key locations, webcams, and satellite views locally.
